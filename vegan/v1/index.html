Our Story

How It Started

In 2020, while the sailing world was standing still, two women from inland Croatia had an idea. Matea Sedlaček — one of the few female skippers on the Adriatic — and her girlfriend Marina Minko, a plant-based chef building her professional career, decided to connect the two things they loved: sailing the Dalmatian islands and eating extraordinarily well.

They launched Croatia's first vegan sailing week with Alvus Sailing. It sold out. The next year sold out. Every year since has sold out.

For 2026, they sail exclusively through Unda Mora Yachting — your private catamaran or sailboat, your crew, your week on the Adriatic.

This is not a retreat. This is not a group tour. This is the original — and the best.

Meet Your Crew

The People Behind Every Extraordinary Week

Matea Sedlaček

Captain & Skipper

Matea is a professional skipper, fitness entrepreneur, and one of a handful of female captains on the Adriatic. Originally from Kutina, she first encountered sailing at the Faculty of Kinesiology in Zagreb — and never looked back. She's been working as a skipper since 2014, was discovered by a German travel agency specialising in sailing, and has since built a reputation as a captain guests trust completely.

She explains the route, offers options, teaches those who want to learn, and handles everything on deck so her guests can simply enjoy being at sea.

"Every week I'm more or less amazed how I, quite small and young, can drive such a big boat. Every successful entry was greeted with applause — because it is something that isn't expected from a woman."

"Our goal is to show people what veganism is really all about — breaking down prejudices about veganism, which is still seen as some sectarian way of life."

Marina Minko

Executive Plant-Based Chef

Marina is a self-taught executive plant-based chef with experience across European restaurant kitchens, vegan cruises, and hotel consulting. She has published 100+ food columns, led professional culinary teams, and built a career around a single conviction: bold, modern plant-based food that genuinely excites guests — including those who've never considered veganism.

Her menus are seasonal, sourced fresh from island markets, and designed to surprise.

"Bold, modern plant-based food that genuinely excites guests — including those who've never considered veganism."
