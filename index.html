Agenda 55 is a 2023 Lagoon 55 catamaran — a symbol of organised elegance, always moving forward with intention and grace. Based in Split, Croatia, she offers an unmatched crewed charter experience along the Dalmatian coast.

With four individually named and designed en-suite staterooms, a professional crew of two, and a comprehensive collection of water toys, Agenda 55 delivers the complete luxury sailing experience. Starlink connectivity, gourmet meals, and the finest Adriatic anchorages — all meticulously planned so you can simply enjoy.

°AGENDA'55" — Age Non-Disclosure. Where quality and experience never age.

Why Agenda 55

Not just a Lagoon 55.
The Lagoon 55.

There are many Lagoon 55s available for charter in Croatia. Agenda 55 is built differently — outfitted, staffed, and operated to a standard that sets it apart from day one.

01 — Privacy
4 cabins. 8 guests. That's it.
Most charter catamarans cram 10–12 guests into 5–6 cabins. Agenda 55 carries a maximum of 8 guests across 4 en-suite staterooms — each uniquely named and designed. Your group has the boat. You don't share it with strangers.
02 — Water Toys
The most equipped fleet in its class.
Williams SportJet 395 (100HP jet tender) · 2× Seabob F5SR · Air e-foil · 2× paddleboards · Wakeboard · Water skis · Donut · Banana · Advanced fishing gear · Snorkeling ×8. Most boats have a tender and two paddleboards. This is a different category.
03 — Crew
Captain + Stewardess
+ Optional Chef
Two permanent professional crew. And if your group wants a private chef for the week, that's available on request. Your meals, your preferences, your pace.
04 — Connectivity
Starlink at sea.
Always on.
High-speed satellite internet throughout the Adriatic. Work remotely, stream, video call — or ignore it completely. The option exists. Most charter boats don't have it.
05 — Built 2023
New vessel.
No compromises.
Delivered in 2023, Agenda 55 has never been a bare-boat. She entered service as a fully crewed luxury charter from day one — fully equipped, fully maintained, and built to perform.
06 — Entertainment
Day and night.
Sound system across flybridge, cockpit, and saloon. Smart TV with Netflix and Disney+. PlayStation 4. Karaoke set. Guitar and synthesizer. Surround sound sunsets and late evenings at anchor — your soundtrack, your mood.
07 — The Name
°AGENDA'55"
"Age Non-Disclosure." A boat that refuses to date itself — built with precision, operated with intent. The degree, minute, and second symbols in the name are coordinates. Every journey on Agenda 55 has a destination, and it's always worth arriving.

Adriatic

Croatia's most extraordinary islands

01
Hvar
The jewel of the Dalmatian coast. Lavender fields, lively harbour nights, secluded coves, and the best sunsets in the Adriatic.
02
Vis
Croatia's remotest and most preserved island. Crystal-clear bays, the famous Blue Cave, untouched nature, and authentic island life.
03
Korčula
The birthplace of Marco Polo. A walled medieval town, excellent local wines, and some of the most dramatic coastline in the Adriatic.
04
Brač
Home to Zlatni Rat — Croatia's most iconic beach — and quiet villages where time moves at the pace of the sea.
05
Kornati
A national park of 89 islands. Wild, unspoiled, and breathtaking — the kind of landscape that reminds you why you came.
06
Šibenik
UNESCO heritage city, gateway to the Krka waterfalls, and a favourite among captains who know where to anchor for the view.
