Why We Exist

Sailing is how the coast was meant to be seen.

Unda Mora grew from a simple belief — that the Adriatic is best experienced slowly, personally, and without the noise of mass tourism. We offer five distinct ways to be on the water. Each one is designed around a different way of living.
