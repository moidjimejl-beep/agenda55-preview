Brand Guidelines · 2026

Unda Mora

These guidelines define how the Unda Mora brand is expressed — across web, print, and every touchpoint. Follow them to maintain consistency and protect the brand's premium positioning.

01 · Logo

The Wave Mark

The Unda Mora logo combines bold geometric sans-serif typography with an integrated wave symbol. The wave represents the breaking sea — the core of everything we do. The O in MORA contains or is replaced by the wave, creating a unique, ownable mark.

04 · Logo Usage

Do & Don't

Do

Use primary logo on white or off-white backgrounds
Use reversed (white) logo on navy or dark photography
Maintain minimum clear space equal to the height of 'U'
Use wave icon alone as favicon or social avatar
Use UM monogram for compact applications (embroidery, small print)

Don't

✗Stretch or distort the logo
✗Change the navy to any other color except white (reversed)
✗Place logo on busy photography without sufficient contrast
✗Add "d.o.o." or legal suffixes to the brand logo
✗Use the teal color for the primary logo mark

05 · Tone of Voice

How We Speak

We are

Quiet. Confident. Precise.

We don't shout. We don't over-promise. We speak like someone who has been on the sea for twenty years and knows exactly what they're talking about.

We are not

Generic. Loud. Transactional.

No "Amazing deals!" No "Book now before it's too late!" We don't sound like a discount travel agency. We sound like a trusted expert.
