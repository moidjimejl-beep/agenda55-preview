The Vessel

Where every journey is purposeful

Agenda 55 is a 2023 Lagoon 55 catamaran — a symbol of organised elegance, always moving forward with intention. Based in Marina Kaštela, Split, she operates crewed charters along the full length of the Dalmatian coast.

Four individually designed en-suite staterooms. A professional captain and stewardess. Optional private chef on request. Starlink internet. A water toy collection that redefines what's possible at sea.

Why Agenda 55

Not just a Lagoon 55. The Lagoon 55.

There are many Lagoon 55s for charter in Croatia. Here's what makes Agenda 55 different.

01 — Privacy
4 cabins. 8 guests. Your boat.
Most charter catamarans carry 10–12 guests. Agenda 55 is capped at 8, across 4 individually named and designed en-suite staterooms. Your group has the vessel — entirely.
02 — Water Toys
The most equipped fleet in its class.
Williams SportJet 395 (100HP) · 2× Seabob F5SR · Air e-foil · Paddleboards · Wakeboard · Water skis · Donut · Banana · Fishing gear · Snorkeling ×8. Most boats offer a tender and a paddleboard. This is a different category entirely.
03 — Crew
Captain + Stewardess + Optional Chef.
Two permanent professional crew — Yachtmaster captain with 30,000+ nautical miles, certified stewardess with daily meals tailored to your group. Private chef available on request.
04 — Connectivity
Starlink at sea. Everywhere.
High-speed satellite internet throughout the Adriatic. Work remotely, stream, stay connected — or don't. The option is there. Most charter vessels still rely on marina Wi-Fi.
05 — New Vessel

Delivered in 2023 and entered service immediately as a fully crewed luxury charter. Never bareboat. Full equipment from day one, maintained to the highest standard throughout.
06 — Entertainment
Day and night, on every deck.
Sound system across flybridge, cockpit, and saloon. Netflix & Disney+. PlayStation 4. Karaoke. Guitar and synthesizer. Sunset cocktails or late evenings at anchor — Agenda 55 sets the scene.
